Left button turns, right button pans, the wheel zooms. Hover the terrain to see coordinates and the distance from the start.

Drag over the terrain with the left button; the stroke is applied when you let go. The right button turns the camera, the middle one pans. An empty radius follows the patch size.

Click the terrain. The outline shows how far the edit reaches; a jump faces down the course.

Grab the terrain with the left button and drag it aside. Inside the yellow circle everything moves as a whole: terrain, objects, rails, AI paths, start and reset points, lights and cameras; out to the orange circle the move fades out and the ground stretches or squeezes.

An empty edge is 2.5 x the move (at least 40 m). The right button turns the camera, the middle one pans. With a turn or a lift, a click is enough.

Click an object (a tree, a building…).

Drag the arrows to move the selected object, or place it elsewhere with a click (it stands on the terrain). R turns it, Delete removes it (moves it under the mountain with its collision). This is how trees and other objects nearby can be reused somewhere new.
